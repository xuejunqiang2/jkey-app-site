Privacy Policy JKEY LIMITED (hereinafter “we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect information when you use jKey, as well as how locally stored wallet data is handled. 1. Information We Process Account Information To register, log in, recover your login password, change your email address, and perform identity verification, we may process your email address, nickname, login credentials, verification codes, and multi‑factor authentication status. Device and Operational Information To identify login devices, secure your account, send notifications and troubleshoot issues, we may process device identifiers, device names or models, system and application versions, push tokens, IP addresses, request timestamps, and necessary operational logs. Wallet and Business Information To provide wallet display, transaction signing, team, approval, collection and notification features, we may process public wallet addresses, public keys, on‑chain transaction information, signed results, order or approval records, and related statuses. Transaction information on public blockchains may be viewable by anyone and is not under our control. Camera and Photo Library With your authorisation, jKey may access your camera or photos you select to recognise QR codes. Camera frames and selected original images are used solely to complete the scanning function; we do not proactively upload them as user data. 2. Sensitive Wallet Data Stored Locally Your mnemonic phrases and private keys are generated or imported, stored, and processed locally on your device when signing is required. The local transaction password is used to protect wallet keys and to verify signing operations. We do not receive, upload, or host your mnemonic phrases, private keys, or local transaction passwords. After signing is completed, in order to submit transactions or complete related business, signed results, public addresses, and transaction data may be sent to our services or to the relevant blockchain network. Please do not share your mnemonic phrases, private keys, transaction password, login password, or verification codes with anyone. 3. Purposes of Information Use - Create and maintain your account, and complete login and identity verification; - Provide wallet, signing, team, approval, collection and notification functions; - Identify abnormal logins, handle security incidents and prevent fraud or abuse; - Respond to enquiries, troubleshoot issues, and improve application stability; - Comply with applicable legal requirements and protect the legitimate interests of users, us, or third parties. 4. Information Sharing and Third‑Party Services We do not sell your personal information. To provide necessary services, we may share required information with infrastructure, push notification and technical service providers within a reasonable scope – for example, sending notifications through the Apple Push Notification service. Such service providers may only process the information for the agreed purposes and must take appropriate protective measures. When you initiate a blockchain transaction, public addresses, transaction content, and signed results may be submitted to the blockchain network. Blockchain data is generally public and difficult to modify or revoke. 5. Data Retention We retain information only for as long as necessary to provide services, ensure security, resolve disputes, and fulfill applicable legal obligations. Retention periods for data stored locally on your device depend on your device, application status, and local operations. Uninstalling the application or clearing device data may result in the inability to recover local wallet materials. 6. Cross‑Border Processing Because our services are available in multiple countries and regions, your information may be processed in jurisdictions other than your own. We will take reasonable measures in accordance with applicable laws to protect the relevant information. 7. Information Security We adopt technical and organisational measures appropriate to the nature of the services to help prevent unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or loss of information. However, no method of network transmission or storage is completely secure. You should also properly protect your device, account, and wallet credentials. 8. Your Choices You may manage camera, photo library, and notification permissions in your system settings. Disabling relevant permissions will not affect unrelated functions, but the corresponding QR‑code scanning, image recognition, or notification features may not be available. You may also contact us using the information below to enquire about the processing of your personal information or to submit requests for rectification, etc. 9. Minors jKey is not intended for persons under 18 years of age. If we become aware that information has been provided in a manner inconsistent with applicable laws, we will handle it as required by law. 10. Policy Updates We may update this policy in response to functional, operational, or legal requirements. Material changes will be notified through in‑app prompts or other reasonable means. The updated policy will take effect as of the stated effective date. 11. Contact Us Email: bd@jkey.org © 2026 JKEY LIMITED. All rights reserved.
